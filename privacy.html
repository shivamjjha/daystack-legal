Privacy Policy

DayStack POS · Effective 22 October 2026 · Updated 25 October 2026

DayStack POS ("DayStack", "we") is an app for business owners. It records menus, orders and sales. Shivam Jha makes and operates DayStack. This policy tells you which data we keep, why we keep it, and what you can do.

Data that we keep

  • Account data: your email address and your password. We do not see your password. Our login provider stores it in a protected (hashed) form.
  • Business data that you enter: dishes, prices, sizes, stock counts, orders, order notes, discounts, void reasons, business name, currency, UPI ID, the name and message for UPI payments, receipt style and the time that your business day starts.
  • Subscription data: your subscription status, the product, the dates of the trial, renewals and refunds, and an Apple transaction number. We do not get your card, bank or UPI details. Apple processes all payments.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your location, contacts or photos.

Data that your customers give

DayStack does not ask for the names or phone numbers of your customers. If you type personal data in an order note, you are responsible for it. A customer pays you through their own UPI app. DayStack only shows a QR code. We do not see or process that payment.

Why we use the data

  • To let you sign in and use the app on your devices.
  • To save and sync your menu, orders and reports.
  • To know if your account has an active subscription, a free trial or free access.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.

Service providers

These companies process data for us, only to run DayStack:

  • Supabase (database and login): stores your account and business data.
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions): gets your DayStack account ID and your Apple purchase records to check your subscription.
  • Apple (App Store payments): processes subscription payments and refunds under Apple's own privacy policy.

These providers can store data on servers outside India.

Data on your iPhone

DayStack keeps a copy of your menu, settings and orders that are not synced yet on your iPhone, so that you can take orders with no internet. Your sign-in token is in the iPhone Keychain. Receipts and exports are made on your iPhone. They leave the iPhone only when you share them.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app: tap Settings › Account › Delete Account. Before you subscribe, tap Delete account on the plan screen. We then delete your account and business data at once, and the app deletes the orders on your iPhone that are not synced yet. Copies in the backups of our service providers are removed when those backups expire. If you cannot open the app, send us an email from the email address of your account, and we delete your account and business data in 30 days or less.

Deleting your account does not cancel your DayStack Pro subscription. Apple continues to charge you until you cancel it: in the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as the law and their own policies require.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app. You can export your orders from the History tab. You can delete your account and data in the app (see "How long we keep data"). You can ask us to correct or delete your data, or ask a question about it. Send an email to sponges.purr.2p@icloud.com from the email address of your account. This address is also the contact for complaints (grievances) under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Security

Data goes between the app and our servers through encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each account read and change only its own data. No system is fully secure. Use a strong password and do not share it.

Children

DayStack is for business owners. It is not for children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we change the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we also tell you in the app.
