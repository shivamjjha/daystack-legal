DayStack POS Support

A point of sale app for food stalls and small shops.

Contact

Send an email to sponges.purr.2p@icloud.com. Write the email address of your DayStack account in the message. We reply in 2 working days or less.

Subscription

  • Plan: DayStack Pro, ₹199 each month. New subscribers get the first 7 days free.
  • Cancel: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. You can also tap Plan › Manage subscription in DayStack. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • Restore a purchase: in DayStack, tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that you used to subscribe.
  • Refunds: Apple makes all refund decisions. In DayStack, tap Plan › Request a Refund, or go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Offer codes: in DayStack, tap Redeem code.

Your data

To get a copy of your data, use Export in the History tab. To delete your account and your data, tap Settings › Account › Delete Account in DayStack. Before you subscribe, tap Delete account on the plan screen. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription (see Cancel above). If you cannot open the app, send us an email from the email address of your account. See the Privacy Policy.
